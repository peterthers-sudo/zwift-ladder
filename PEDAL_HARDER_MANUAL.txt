PEDAL HARDER — GAME MANUAL
==========================
Zwift Ladder Race Simulator · 5 vs 5
Version 1.2.5


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You manage a 5-rider LEQP team in a simulated Zwift ladder race against
an opponent team of 5 riders. The race plays out tick by tick (1 tick =
approx. 1 second of real time). You set effort levels, trigger attacks
and powerups, and give captain commands. Most rider behaviour is
automatic — your decisions alter the outcome at key moments.

Win condition: the team with the most combined ladder points wins.
Points per finish position: 1st=10, 2nd=9, 3rd=8 ... 10th=1.
In a 5v5 race the lowest possible finish position is 10th.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUTES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Only routes with a saved elevation profile are available. Each route is
divided into sections: Flat, Climb, Punch, and Sprint. The profile is
shown as a coloured bar above the canvas during the race.

A normal rider at 100% stamina and Tempo effort finishes any route in
approximately 130 ticks. Stronger riders finish faster; a rider at Hard
effort throughout finishes in roughly 100-110 ticks.

The race has a safety valve of 400 ticks — any rider still racing at
that point is marked DNF.

You choose both the route and the number of laps before each race.
The opponent has no say in route selection — you are the challenger.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STAMINA
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every rider has a stamina bar (0–100%). Stamina determines how fast
they ride at any given effort level.

Speed modifier from stamina:
  speed × (0.55 + stamina% × 0.45)

At 100% stamina: full speed (modifier 1.00)
At   0% stamina: 55% of maximum speed (modifier 0.55)

Stamina states shown on rider cards:
  🟢 Fresh      > 75%
  🟡 Good       > 50%
  🟠 Tired      > 25%
  🔴 Cracked    >  8%
  💀 Blown up   ≤  8%  — rider automatically drops to Rest effort

Stamina change per tick by effort level:
  Rest   (💤) : +1.5  (recovery)
  Easy   (🚴) : −0.3
  Tempo  (🎯) : −1.0
  Hard   (🔥) : −2.5
  Attack (⚡) : −7.0


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EFFORT LEVELS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You set effort for each of your own riders manually. Opponent riders
are controlled by AI. Effort affects both speed and stamina drain.

  Level 1 — Rest   💤   Speed ×0.75   Stamina +1.5/tick
  Level 2 — Easy   🚴   Speed ×0.88   Stamina −0.3/tick
  Level 3 — Tempo  🎯   Speed ×1.00   Stamina −1.0/tick  (baseline)
  Level 4 — Hard   🔥   Speed ×1.12   Stamina −2.5/tick
  Level 5 — Attack ⚡   Speed ×1.26   Stamina −7.0/tick

Tempo (3) is the reference: a "normal" rider at full stamina and Tempo
effort will finish a route in approximately 130 ticks.

Tip: Hard burns stamina 2.5× faster than Tempo. Reserve Hard for
climbs and decisive moments. Use Rest to recover a cracked rider.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RIDER ROLES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Each rider is assigned a role before the race on the Strategy screen.
The role affects how the rider's AI behaves automatically when you are
not overriding it with a captain command.

LEADER ⭐
  The team rides for the leader. Their goal is the best finish
  position. They receive a speed bonus from domestiques who protect
  them. AI pushes Hard on climbs when fresh, picks up pace near the
  finish.

SPRINTER ⚡
  Conserves energy throughout the race. AI holds Easy or Tempo for
  the first 85% of the route, then goes full gas in the final sprint.
  Strong in flat finishes, weak on long climbs.

DOMESTIQUE 🛡
  Automatically protects the leader the entire race. Gives the leader
  a speed boost as long as the domestique has stamina > 25%.
  The protect button has no effect for leader or sprinter roles
  (they never protect).

CLIMBER ⛰
  Pushes Hard on climb and punch sections when fresh. Falls back to
  Tempo or Easy on flats. Strong on mountain routes.

WILD CARD 🦅
  Attacks opportunistically. AI triggers attacks when the rider is
  fresh and stronger than the opposing leader.

  Note: Wild Card is a role (AI behaviour), not a rider type. The
  terrain bonus a Wild Card rider gets depends on their rider TYPE
  (Diesel/Climber/Puncheur/Sprinter), which is derived from their
  w/kg statistics — see TERRAIN section below.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TERRAIN — SECTION FIT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Each route is divided into sections: Flat, Climb, Punch, and Sprint.
Riders get a terrain speed bonus depending on their TYPE:

  Flat:   Diesel    +5%,  Climber   −3%
  Climb:  Climber  +10%
  Punch:  Puncheur  +9%
  Sprint: Sprinter +12%

Rider TYPE (Diesel, Climber, Puncheur, Sprinter) is derived
automatically from their real w/kg statistics. This is separate from
the rider's ROLE (Leader, Sprinter, Domestique, etc.) — both a ROLE
and a TYPE exist simultaneously for every rider.

  Sprinter : 15s w/kg / 5min w/kg > 2.8
  Climber  : 20min w/kg > 4.2 AND 5min w/kg > 4.8
  Puncheur : 1min w/kg / 20min w/kg > 1.6
  Diesel   : everyone else

The rider's actual w/kg and watt values from Zwift are also used
directly in the physics speed calculation — stronger riders (higher
w/kg) are genuinely faster, not just categorised differently.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ATTACK
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Triggered by the ⚡ Attack button on a rider's card, or via the
captain command "Everyone attack!".

Effect:
  • Rider is forced to effort 5 (Attack) for 3 ticks
  • After 3 ticks: forced to effort 4 (Hard) for 4 more ticks
  • The rider is ready to ride normally again after those 7 ticks
  • Cooldown: 15 ticks before the rider can attack again

Requirements to trigger manually:
  • Stamina ≥ 30%
  • Not currently attacking, in hard phase, doing a Hail Mary, or
    on cooldown

Captain "Everyone attack!" triggers all eligible riders simultaneously.

AI opponents attack automatically when fresh (stamina > 72%),
stronger than your leader, and random chance (≈ 1.8% per tick).


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HAIL MARY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Triggered by the 💀 Hail Mary button on a rider's card.

Effect:
  • Rider is forced to effort 5 (Attack) continuously
  • Continues until stamina drops to ≤ 8% (blown up)
  • Once blown up, the rider is automatically forced to Rest
  • No cooldown — the rider is already spent after a Hail Mary

Use case: A last-ditch move in the final kilometres to squeeze out
a better finishing position at the cost of riding completely empty.
There is no recovery — the rider will be very slow for the remainder.

Requirements to trigger:
  • Stamina ≥ 30%
  • Not currently attacking or on cooldown


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MARK & FOLLOW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You can order one of your riders to shadow a specific opponent by
clicking directly on the canvas.

How it works — two clicks:
  1. Click an opponent rider on the canvas
       → A gold blinking ring appears around them (lasts 3 seconds).
         The comms feed confirms: "Marking X — click your riders
         to follow!"

  2. While the target is marked, click one of your own riders
       → Your rider starts following that opponent. A dashed gold
         ring and a ▶ arrow appear above your rider's circle.

Your following rider automatically mirrors the opponent's effort
level, with a small bonus on climbs and punches. The strategy keeps
your rider in the opponent's wheel — useful for covering dangerous
opponents or staying in contact with a break.

To stop following:
  • Click your own rider when no target is marked
      → The rider stops following and returns to normal AI/manual.
  • Setting a manual effort (buttons 1–4) also cancels following.
  • Following is cancelled automatically if the target drops out
    or abandons the race.

Note: following does NOT prevent blown-up or stamina management.
A rider who runs out of stamina will still be forced to Rest even
while following.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PROTECT / SHIELD
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The 🛡 Protect button toggles whether a rider shields the leader.

Effect while protecting:
  • The protecting rider rides slightly more economically
  • The leader receives a speed bonus of +0.004 progress/tick per
    protecting domestique (roughly equivalent to a small gear change)
  • Multiple domestiques stacks — 3 protecting riders = 3× the bonus

Stops working automatically when:
  • The protecting rider's stamina drops below 25%

Protect is disabled for leader and sprinter (they never protect).
Domestiques auto-protect at race start; you can turn it off manually.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
POWERUPS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Powerups spawn on the track as icons in rider lanes. A rider collects
one automatically when they pass over it — but only if they don't
already hold one and have no active powerup running.

Collected powerups are shown on the rider card. They do NOT activate
automatically — you must press the button or use "Use powerups".

Once activated, a rider cannot collect a new powerup until the active
effect expires.

AERO BOOST 💨       +20% speed on flat sections         10 ticks
FEATHERWEIGHT ☁     +30% speed on climb/punch sections  10 ticks
STEAMROLLER ⚡      +15% speed on all terrain            10 ticks
ENERGY GEL 🍬       +40 stamina instantly (no duration)
PEDAL HARDER 🔥     +35% speed on all terrain             3 ticks
                    Rare drop — 20% spawn chance vs
                    normal powerups

Opponent riders use powerups automatically with contextual timing:
  Gel       — when stamina < 45%
  Aero      — on flat, after 30% of route
  Feather   — on climb, after 20% of route
  Steamroller — after 65% of route
  Pedal Harder — after 72% of route, 25% chance per tick


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CAPTAIN COMMANDS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Captain commands affect the whole team simultaneously.

🚴 Stay in draft    Set all riders to Easy (effort 2)
🎯 Hold tempo       Set all riders to Tempo (effort 3)
🔥 Go hard          Set all riders to Hard (effort 4)
⚡ Everyone attack! All eligible riders trigger an attack burst
🛡 Protect leader   All non-leader/sprinter riders start protecting
💤 Everyone rest    Set all riders to Rest (effort 1)
💬 Give status      All riders report their current stamina status

💥 Use powerups     All riders who hold an unused powerup activate it
                    simultaneously

🔥 Pedal Harder!    ONE-TIME USE PER RACE
                    Captain shouts "PEDAL HARDER!!!"
                    All active riders get a Pedal Harder boost (3 ticks,
                    +35% speed). Each rider responds in the comms feed.
                    Button grays out after use.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRE-RACE STRATEGY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Chosen on the Strategy screen before the race starts.

Control         Normal AI pace management. No special team plan.

Sprint Start    All riders burst at full gas (effort 5) for 3 ticks,
                then push Hard (effort 4) for 4 more ticks — gaps the
                field early without blowing up. The team can continue
                normally after those 7 ticks.

Go Hard on      Adaptive climb effort. Short climbs (< 8% of route):
Climbs          always Hard. Long climbs: Hard when fresh (> 75%
                stamina), Tempo when stamina fades — so the team
                doesn't blow up before the top. Best on mountain
                routes with multiple climbs.

Sprint Finish   Sprinters hold Easy/Tempo until 85% of the route,
                then go full gas. All other riders push Hard in the
                final 20% of the race.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRE-RACE INTEL
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Before each race, random events may appear in the countdown overlay.
They affect starting conditions — roughly 0–2 events per race.

Events that can occur (for either team):

  ⏰ Late arrival        One rider starts 2–6 ticks into the race
  😴 Tired legs          1–2 riders start at 75% stamina
  📡 Connection issue    One rider drops out mid-race, reconnects 10
                         ticks later at the same position
  ❌ Absent rider        One rider is withdrawn — team races with 4
  🚲 Forgot to switch    One rider has 5% less power the entire race
     bikes

Events can hit your team or the opponent team. Probability per event:
  Opp late arrival   14%    Own late arrival    8%
  Opp tired legs     13%    Own tired legs      12%
  Opp drops mid-race 10%    Own absent           7%
  Opp absent          7%    Own forgot bike      8%
  Opp forgot bike    10%


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT YOU CONTROL vs. WHAT IS AUTOMATIC
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

YOU CONTROL:
  • Effort level for each of your 5 riders (1–4, not 5 — that's Attack)
  • When to trigger an attack (⚡) per rider
  • When to trigger a Hail Mary (💀) per rider
  • When to activate a powerup per rider
  • Whether a rider protects the leader (🛡 toggle)
  • All captain commands (timing is your decision)
  • Pedal Harder! (once per race, you choose when)
  • Rider roles and race strategy (chosen before the race)
  • Route and laps selection
  • Which 5 riders from your squad race

AUTOMATIC (AI handles):
  • All opponent rider decisions (effort, attacks, powerups, timing)
  • Domestiques auto-protects at race start (you can override)
  • Sprinter automatically saves legs and sprints at end (if Sprint
    Finish strategy is active)
  • Riders auto-switch to Rest when blown up (stamina ≤ 8%)
  • Rider chat / speech bubbles
  • Powerup pickup from track (when rider passes over spawn point)
  • Grouping and gap calculation
  • Rider coasting after crossing the finish line
  • Position tracking and results


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CAMPAIGN — LADDER RANKING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The campaign tracks your team's position in the real Zwift Cycling
Ladder standings. Data is updated nightly from live standings.

CHALLENGING
  You can only challenge teams ranked 1–7 positions above you.
  Example: if you are ranked #53, you can challenge teams #46–#52.
  The opponent dropdown shows only eligible teams.

WINNING
  If you win, you advance to the opponent's position.
  Example: beat #46 → you become #46, they drop to #47.
  You can then challenge #39–#45.

LOSING
  Your rank does not change on a loss. The consecutive loss counter
  goes up (shown as ⚠️ 1/3, 2/3 in the campaign banner).

3 CONSECUTIVE LOSSES
  Campaign resets: you return to your starting position, all wins
  are cleared, and all accumulated win bonuses are removed.

MILESTONES
  Crossing these thresholds for the first time triggers a splash:
    Top 50 · Top 25 · Top 10 · #3 Podium · #2 Runner-Up · #1 Champion
  #1 includes confetti animation.

WIN BONUS
  After each win, riders earn a small w/kg bonus based on how many
  positions were gained and their individual finish position:

    Ranks climbed × position factor × 0.008 w/kg

  Position factors:
    1st  → ×1.00      4th–5th → ×0.40
    2nd  → ×0.80      6th–7th → ×0.22
    3rd  → ×0.60      8th–10th → ×0.10

  Example: win against a team 7 positions above, rider finishes 1st:
    7 × 1.00 × 0.008 = +0.056 w/kg

  The bonus accumulates across wins, is shown on rider cards as
  "+X.XX w/kg", and is applied directly in physics calculations
  (getFlatPower). It is cleared on campaign reset.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Positions 1–10 score ladder points:
  1st=10  2nd=9  3rd=8  4th=7  5th=6  6th=5  7th=4  8th=3  9th=2  10th=1

Combined points from your 5 riders vs opponent's 5 riders determine
the result. Most points wins. Equal points = draw (no rank change).

DNF riders (did not finish within time limit) score 0 points and are
listed last, ranked by progress reached.
