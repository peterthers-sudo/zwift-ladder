Overview

You manage a 5-rider LEQP team in a simulated Zwift ladder race against an opponent team of 5 riders. The race plays out tick by tick (1 tick ≈ 1 second of real time). You set effort levels, trigger attacks and powerups, and give captain commands. Most rider behaviour is automatic — your decisions alter the outcome at key moments.

Win condition: the team with the most combined ladder points wins. Points per finish position: 1st=10, 2nd=9 … 10th=1. In a 5v5 race the lowest possible finish is 10th.

Routes

Only routes with a saved elevation profile are available. Each route is divided into sections: Flat, Climb, Punch, and Sprint. The profile is shown as a coloured bar above the canvas during the race.

A normal rider at 100% stamina and Tempo effort finishes in approximately 130 ticks. Stronger riders or Hard effort finishes in ~100–110 ticks. The race has a safety valve of 400 ticks — any rider still racing is marked DNF. You choose both route and laps; the opponent has no say.

Stamina

Every rider has a stamina bar (0–100%). Stamina determines speed via: speed × (0.55 + stamina% × 0.45). At 100% stamina: full speed (×1.00). At 0%: 55% of max speed (×0.55).

🟢 Fresh > 75% 🟡 Good > 50% 🟠 Tired > 25% 🔴 Cracked > 8% 💀 Blown up ≤ 8% — rider forced to Rest

Stamina change per tick:

Rest 💤 +1.5/tick (recovery) Easy 🚴 −0.3/tick Tempo 🎯 −1.0/tick Hard 🔥 −2.5/tick Attack ⚡ −7.0/tick

Effort Levels

You set effort for each of your own riders manually (buttons 1–4 on their card). Opponents are AI-controlled.

1 — Rest 💤 Speed ×0.75 Stamina +1.5/tick 2 — Easy 🚴 Speed ×0.88 Stamina −0.3/tick 3 — Tempo 🎯 Speed ×1.00 Stamina −1.0/tick (baseline) 4 — Hard 🔥 Speed ×1.12 Stamina −2.5/tick 5 — Attack ⚡ Speed ×1.26 Stamina −7.0/tick (not manually selectable)

Tip: Hard burns stamina 2.5× faster than Tempo. Reserve it for climbs and decisive moments. Use Rest to recover a cracked rider.

Rider Roles

Roles are assigned on the Strategy screen and control AI behaviour when you're not overriding with a captain command.

  • Leader ⭐ — The team rides for the leader. AI pushes Hard on climbs when fresh, lifts pace near the finish. Receives speed bonus from protecting domestiques.
  • Sprinter ⚡ — Holds Easy/Tempo for the first 85% of the route, then goes full gas. Strong on flat finishes, weak on long climbs.
  • Domestique 🛡 — Automatically protects the leader the entire race, giving them a speed bonus as long as stamina > 25%.
  • Climber ⛰ — Pushes Hard on climb and punch sections when fresh. Falls back to Tempo or Easy on flats.
  • Wild Card 🦅 — Attacks opportunistically when fresh and stronger than the opposing leader. Wild Card is a role (AI behaviour), not a rider type — see Terrain below.

Terrain — Section Fit

Rider type (derived from real w/kg statistics) gives terrain speed bonuses. This is separate from the rider's role.

Flat: Diesel +5%, Climber −3% Climb: Climber +10% Punch: Puncheur +9% Sprint: Sprinter +12% Classification rules: Sprinter : 15s w/kg ÷ 5min w/kg > 2.8 Climber : 20min w/kg > 4.2 AND 5min w/kg > 4.8 Puncheur : 1min w/kg ÷ 20min w/kg > 1.6 Diesel : everyone else

The rider's actual w/kg values are also used directly in the physics speed calculation — stronger riders are genuinely faster.

Attack

Triggered by the ⚡ Attack button on a rider's card, or via "Everyone attack!" captain command.

  • Rider is forced to effort 5 (Attack) for 3 ticks
  • Then forced to effort 4 (Hard) for 4 more ticks
  • Ready to ride normally again after those 7 ticks total
  • Cooldown: 15 ticks before the rider can attack again

Requirements: stamina ≥ 30%, not currently attacking/hard phase/Hail Mary/cooldown. AI opponents attack when stamina > 72%, stronger than your leader, ~1.8% chance per tick.

Hail Mary

Triggered by the 💀 Hail Mary button on a rider's card.

  • Rider is forced to effort 5 continuously until stamina ≤ 8% (blown up)
  • Once blown up, automatically forced to Rest — no recovery possible
  • No cooldown, but the rider will be very slow for the remainder

Use case: a last-ditch move in the final kilometres to squeeze out a better finishing position at the cost of riding completely empty. Requirements: stamina ≥ 30%, not already attacking.

Mark & Follow

Order one of your riders to shadow a specific opponent by clicking directly on the canvas — two clicks:

  1. Click an opponent rider → a gold blinking ring appears around them (lasts 3 seconds). Comms confirms: "Marking X — click your riders to follow!"
  2. While target is marked, click one of your own riders → your rider follows. A dashed gold ring and ▶ arrow appear above their circle.

Your following rider mirrors the opponent's effort level (with a small bonus on climbs). To stop following: click your own rider when no target is marked, or set a manual effort (buttons 1–4). Following cancels automatically if the target drops out or abandons.

Protect / Shield

The 🛡 Protect button toggles whether a rider shields the leader.

  • Leader receives +0.004 progress/tick per protecting domestique (stacks — 3 domestiques = 3× bonus)
  • Stops automatically when the protecting rider's stamina drops below 25%
  • Disabled for leader and sprinter roles (they never protect)

Domestiques auto-protect at race start; you can turn it off manually.

Powerups

Spawn on the track as icons. A rider collects one automatically when passing over it (if not already holding one). Collected powerups do NOT activate automatically — press the button on the rider card, or use "Use powerups" captain command.

💨 Aero Boost +20% speed on flat sections 10 ticks ☁ Featherweight +30% speed on climb/punch sections 10 ticks ⚡ Steamroller +15% speed on all terrain 10 ticks 🍬 Energy Gel +40 stamina instantly 🔥 Pedal Harder +35% speed on all terrain 3 ticks Rare — 20% spawn chance

Opponents use powerups contextually: Gel when stamina < 45%, Aero on flat after 30%, Feather on climb after 20%, Steamroller after 65%, Pedal Harder after 72% with 25% chance per tick.

Captain Commands

Affect the whole team simultaneously.

🚴 Stay in draft All riders → Easy (effort 2) 🎯 Hold tempo All riders → Tempo (effort 3) 🔥 Go hard All riders → Hard (effort 4) ⚡ Everyone attack! All eligible riders trigger an attack burst 🛡 Protect leader All non-leader/sprinter riders start protecting 💤 Everyone rest All riders → Rest (effort 1) 💬 Give status All riders report current stamina 💥 Use powerups All riders with held powerup activate simultaneously 🔥 Pedal Harder! ONE-TIME USE — all active riders get +35% speed for 3 ticks. Button grays out after use.

Pre-race Strategy

  • Control — Normal AI pace management. No special plan.
  • Sprint Start — All riders burst at effort 5 for 3 ticks, then Hard for 4 more — gaps the field without blowing up.
  • Go Hard on Climbs — Adaptive: short climbs (< 8% of route) always Hard; long climbs → Hard when fresh (> 75% stamina), Tempo when stamina fades.
  • Sprint Finish — Sprinters hold Easy/Tempo until 85% of the route, then full gas. Other riders push Hard in the final 20%.

Pre-race Intel

Random events appear in the countdown overlay, affecting starting conditions (~0–2 per race). Events can hit either team.

⏰ Late arrival One rider starts 2–6 ticks late 😴 Tired legs 1–2 riders start at 75% stamina 📡 Connection issue One rider drops briefly, reconnects 10 ticks later ❌ Absent rider One rider withdrawn — team races with 4 🚲 Forgot to switch One rider has 5% less power the entire race

What You Control vs. Automatic

You control: effort per rider (1–4), attacks, Hail Mary, powerup activation, protect toggle, all captain commands, Pedal Harder! timing, rider roles, strategy, route & laps, which 5 riders race.

Automatic (AI): all opponent decisions, domestiques auto-protect at start, sprinter saves legs for sprint finish, riders auto-Rest when blown up, powerup pickup, grouping & gap calculation, position tracking, coasting after finish.

Campaign — Ladder Ranking

Tracks your team's position in the real Zwift Cycling Ladder standings (updated nightly).

  • Challenging: you can only challenge teams ranked 1–7 positions above you.
  • Winning: you advance to the opponent's position.
  • Losing: rank unchanged. Consecutive loss counter goes up (⚠️ 1/3, 2/3).
  • 3 consecutive losses: campaign resets — back to starting position, all wins and bonuses cleared.
  • Milestones: Top 50 · Top 25 · Top 10 · #3 Podium · #2 Runner-Up · #1 Champion (confetti).

Win Bonus: ranks climbed × position factor × 0.008 w/kg per rider, accumulated across wins. Position factors: 1st ×1.00, 2nd ×0.80, 3rd ×0.60, 4th–5th ×0.40, 6th–7th ×0.22, 8th–10th ×0.10. Cleared on campaign reset.

Scoring

1st=10 2nd=9 3rd=8 4th=7 5th=6 6th=5 7th=4 8th=3 9th=2 10th=1

Combined points from your 5 riders vs opponent's 5 riders. Most points wins. Equal points = draw (no rank change). DNF riders score 0 and are listed last by progress reached.
